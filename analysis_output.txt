Conventional linear and rule-based soil erosion susceptibility models often struggle to capture this complexity, particularly under real-world conditions where environmental data are noisy, heterogeneous, and incomplete. To address these limitations, this study proposes an unsupervised deep learning–based framework capable of learning generalized and previously unseen patterns of soil erosion risk. The methodology is applied to five woredas—Ankober, Menjar, Kewet, Merabet, and Menze Gera—in the Amhara Regional State of Ethiopia.
